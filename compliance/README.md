# Compliance and Regulations

This section covers regulatory requirements as architecture inputs. You won't be asked to recite laws in an interview, but knowing how compliance shapes design decisions is a senior-level signal.

## Topics in This Section

- [GDPR Compliance in System Design](gdpr-compliance.md) - Data subject rights, erasure, and privacy by design
- [Data Privacy Patterns](data-privacy.md) - PII handling, encryption, tokenization, and retention

## Why This Matters in Interviews

- "Where does user data live, and who can access it?" is now a standard follow-up
- Data residency questions change your storage and partitioning strategy
- Retention and deletion requirements affect your event and backup design
